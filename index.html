Maybe Box.

A little chance. A lot of joy.

No batteries. No apps. Your dog pushes a paddle, kibble bounces through a field of pegs, and an odds dial sets how often a piece drops out. Pure mechanics, pure surprise.

See the Maybe Box

How it works.

1

The paddle

Your dog nudges the paddle with its nose or paw. A single push tips the hopper and releases one piece of kibble.

2

Bouncing kibble

The kibble tumbles down through a grid of pegs, bouncing left and right. No two drops take the same path.

3

The odds dial

Turn the dial to set how often a piece actually drops into the bowl. More chance, more anticipation, more joy.
