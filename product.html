Safety notes

  • Supervise your dog's initial sessions.
  • Dry kibble only.
  • Discontinue use if the box cracks or any part breaks.
  • Not suitable for dogs that chew through hard plastic.
  • Not a replacement for exercise or separation-anxiety treatment.
